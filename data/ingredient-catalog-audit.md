# Ingredient catalog audit

Generated by `make ingredient-catalog-audit` (also run by `make pipeline`). Do not edit by hand.

Replays the three ingredient-name matchers over committed corpora:

- **Nutrition**: `nutrition::estimate`. "Name recognized" means the name matched a food; "calories computed" also needs a usable quantity. A recipe is fully estimated when every line has calories computed, and has per-serving calories when it is also fully estimated and its servings parse; only snapshots carry servings.
- **Density**: `find_density`, over lines with a volume unit.
- **Shopping category**: `categorize`; "categorized" means not "Other".

Unrecognized names are lowercased with whitespace collapsed.
