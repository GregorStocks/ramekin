Rice Bowl with Fresh Tomato Salsa

A weeknight rice bowl topped with a bright, quick salsa.

Rice Bowl
1 cup rice
2 cups water
1 teaspoon salt

Cook the rice in the water with the salt until tender and all the liquid is absorbed.

Fresh Tomato Salsa
2 tomatoes, diced
1/4 cup chopped onion
1 tablespoon lime juice

Combine the salsa ingredients and let them sit for ten minutes before spooning over the rice.
