Apple Pie

For the crust
2 cups flour
1 stick butter
1/4 cup ice water

For the filling
6 apples
1 cup sugar
1 teaspoon cinnamon

Make the crust by cutting butter into flour. Roll out and place in pie dish.

Slice apples and toss with sugar and cinnamon. Fill the crust and bake at 375 for 45 minutes.
